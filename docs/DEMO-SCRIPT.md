# Line: one-minute demo script

Draft 1. A 60-second edit with 114 spoken words, including three chat-to-app demonstrations and a closing invitation to imagine more uses.

The central idea is friends making useful software together through conversation. Party planning shows collaboration; morning check-ins show competition and accountability; the boba list shows shared information. The final nine-second vision segment connects those capabilities to future possibilities.

## Chat lines for capture

Record each scenario separately. These are suggested human messages. Astra's lines below describe the desired response; use its actual reply in the recording. The party segment establishes the clarification flow. The other two use explicit build requests so the edit can spend more time inside the apps.

### Party

Start with these two messages already visible:

- Sam: "Party at mine this weekend?"
- Jo: "I'm in. Who's bringing what?"

Then record:

- Alex: "@Astra help us organise the party?"
- Astra: a short proposal such as "Want a shared list where everyone claims what they're bringing?"
- Sam: "@Astra yes, make it."
- Astra/build card: the returned party link.

Open the link. Claim an unassigned supply item and hold on the resulting name. Keep other friends' assignments visible. Use the generated app's actual item names and controls.

### Morning challenge

Start with these two messages already visible:

- Alex: "Snoozed my alarm again."
- Sam: "We need some competition."

Then record:

- Jo: "@Astra build us a morning check-in leaderboard."
- Astra/build card: the returned morning link.

Open the link. Use a participant who has not checked in today. Tap `I'm up` once, then hold on the visible result. Seeded streaks can remain visible; don't imply the recording demonstrates several days passing.

### Boba list

Start with these two messages already visible:

- Jo: "Boba run?"
- Alex: "Yes! Everyone's order gets lost here."

Then record:

- Sam: "@Astra make us a shared boba order list."
- Astra/build card: the returned boba link.

Open the link. Have two friends' choices already present, choose the third drink, and hold on the completed group summary. The demonstrated outcome is an organised list for ordering.

## Capture and pacing

- Record the voiceover first. At 135 words per minute, 114 words take about 51 seconds before pauses. Use the time markers for breaths and visual holds; time a real read before locking the edit.
- Capture each chat and app as a separate take, with a few seconds of still footage before and after each action. Trim those handles in the edit.
- Preload the ordinary chat context. Cut typing, loading, tab switching, and generation waits. Keep the request, returned link, and resulting app visibly connected. Use a clear cut across the generation wait; the narration makes no build-speed claim.
- Give each app one main interaction and a readable result. Keep the camera crop and cursor movement steady. Prefer a close crop of the relevant UI to a whole desktop shot.
- Keep added text to the logo, the capability labels, and the closing question/tagline. If captions are needed, use short phrases timed to speech rather than paragraphs.
- Keep all audio and transitions inside the 60-second timeline. Finish speaking by 00:59 and export no longer than 01:00.
